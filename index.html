The Journey Between Two Schools of Law

My education in law has been more than a journey between classrooms. It has been a journey through people, places, cultures, languages, challenges, and the many realities of Ethiopia.

01

Injibara University

Amhara Regional State • School of Law

My journey in higher education began at Injibara University, where I spent approximately three years studying law.

Moving to Injibara was more than simply joining a university. It opened the door to a new environment, new communities, new languages, and new ways of understanding society.

Three years. One university. Countless lessons.
02

The Road Became a Classroom

The journey between Injibara and home became more than a physical distance. It became an opportunity to observe Ethiopia beyond the boundaries of a university campus.

I travelled through different parts of the country and encountered communities with different languages, cultures, norms, values, economic circumstances, and traditions.

On my final journey back, I travelled through Benishangul-Gumuz and had the opportunity to visit and witness the area of the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD).

“Sometimes the road between two destinations teaches you more than the destination itself.”
03

Jimma University

Jimma • School of Law

My journey eventually brought me to Jimma University, where I continued my legal education.

Jimma is especially meaningful to me because it is my birthplace. Coming to Jimma was therefore not simply a transfer from one university to another.

A return to my roots. A continuation of the journey.
04

More Than Two Schools

Injibara University and Jimma University represent two important chapters of my academic life.

But my education has never been limited to two campuses. The roads I travelled, the people I encountered, the languages I heard, and the cultures I experienced have all become part of my education.

These experiences have changed the way I understand law. I have learned that law is not only found in books, lecture halls, statutes, and courtrooms.

Law lives among people.

Two Schools. One Journey. Many Lessons.

Two universities gave me classrooms. Ethiopia gave me the wider lesson.
